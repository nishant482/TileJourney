Full description:
Find a quiet moment with Tile Journey, a triple tile matching puzzle game from Smart Axis.

Tap uncovered tiles to move them into your tray. Match three identical tiles to clear them, then keep matching until the board is empty. Plan your moves: the tray holds seven tiles, and covered tiles become available as you remove the layers above them.

A LITTLE ADVENTURE IN EVERY MATCH
Explore 3,000 seeded puzzles with layered boards and a colorful collection of fruit, treats and nature-inspired tile symbols. Each puzzle has a legal completion path, while your choices decide how the tray fills.

SCENERY FOR YOUR MOOD
Choose from 45 scenery options: the 25 earlier themes plus 20 new illustrated destinations, including cities, islands, gardens and ocean escapes. Use automatic scenery to change the backdrop as your journey progresses, or keep a favorite theme.

HELP WHEN YOU NEED IT
Undo a move, highlight an available tile with a hint, or shuffle the remaining tiles. Earn coins through first-time level completion and collect a daily gift for extra boosters. Retry a puzzle at your own pace.

PLAY YOUR WAY
Choose from 20 beautiful tile collections, including flowers, fruit, emoji, gems, candy, animals, ocean friends and more. Watch matching tiles fly together, combine and burst into falling marble-like particles while you keep playing. Play at your own pace or enable a three-minute timed challenge for new puzzles. Enjoy gentle matching sound effects, turn sound or vibration on or off, and personalize your explorer name. A timer is optional. No account or online purchase is required. Settings include device-local rating feedback and sharing via the Android/browser share chooser.

YOUR PROGRESS STAYS ON YOUR DEVICE
Your active puzzle, levels, coins, boosters and settings are saved locally. The Android game works offline. The browser version works offline after its first successful load. Clearing app or browser data removes saved progress; cross-device syncing is not included.

Match three. Clear the board. Find your calm with Tile Journey.
